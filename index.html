Three promises in your ads. One home screen for all of them.

Day 1

Pass the campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the Google Ads campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Scan-and-solve, learn-it-right and beat-your-time each get their own first screens; organic installs keep today’s home until you choose to split them.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on purchases, refunds and what each install from that ad earns, ads included, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. Your Google advertiser account runs ads for many apps; we used only the ones for Rubik's Cube Solver. We found no Meta ads and no Apple Ads: the app is on Google Play only. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
